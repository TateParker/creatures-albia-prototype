# New Albia — Godot 3D Slice

This native 3D project runs alongside the browser prototype. It targets Godot 4.7.2-stable Standard and keeps the browser version playable as the cognition reference.

## Open and run

Open this `godot/` folder in Godot 4.7.2-stable Standard, or launch it from PowerShell:

```powershell
Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --path .\godot
```

Run the command examples in this guide from the repository root; they use `--path .\godot` so `res://` paths resolve against the native Godot project.

The current vertical slice starts with two independently simulated Norns, Mallow and Moss, in one connected habitat spanning Sunmeadow, Softwood Grove, and Glassfall Hollow. Honey fruit, berries, a spring, spinner, drawing station, echo bloom, and local curiosity trail markers give them different nearby needs and interaction outcomes. The local cognition and biochemistry systems support forage, drink, explore, player guidance, Praise/Scold, typed vocabulary teaching, family pairing, and JSON save/load. The living roster can grow to four Norns including unhatched eggs. All living Norns continue to act while one is focused. A Norn with high social attention can approach and watch another; witnessed resource use, play, exploration, Praise, and Scold events can change its learning, chemistry, and remembered moments.

The expanded environment remains a code-built blockout. Meshy generated textured placeholders for 14 model families; their prompts, task receipts, topology candidates, and review notes are documented in [`ASSET_INVENTORY.md`](ASSET_INVENTORY.md) and [`../assets/generated/meshy-prompts.md`](../assets/generated/meshy-prompts.md). The approved static Norn model is admitted at `res://assets/generated/norns/albia-norn/model.glb`. `CharacterBody3D` continues to drive locomotion and the scripted drawing effector remains active; the generated model has no rig or skin. The original procedural geometry builder stays as a load fallback. The new egg is a review candidate and does not replace the runtime incubation visuals.

## Controls

- Choose a living Norn from the HUD roster to focus them. Direct player actions and the HUD's age, stage, needs, biochemistry, vocabulary, and remembered moments follow the focused Norn; everyone else keeps simulating.
- Use **Guide to fruit**, **Guide to spinner**, **Guide to berries**, **Guide to spring**, or **Guide to echo bloom** to direct the focused Norn for up to seven in-game seconds.
- **Praise** or **Scold** becomes available after the focused Norn eats, forages, drinks, plays, or explores. One response can be applied within 12 in-game seconds.
- Select **Teach word**, type one word (up to 24 characters), then choose the matching target button, **Teach Praise**, or **Teach Scold**. Vocabulary belongs to the focused Norn. Teaching the same word and meaning again builds confidence; the Norn uses it in the status when confidence reaches 0.60. A word cannot be reassigned to another meaning.
- Outside Teach mode, Praise raises the matching target word confidence by 0.14; Scold lowers it by 0.14. Teaching Praise/Scold words does not apply behavior feedback.
- Choose **Biochemistry** to open the read-only, scrollable inspector. It shows all 19 named compound concentrations and up to six largest non-zero reaction fluxes from the latest chemistry step. The compact Needs/Body summary stays on the HUD and includes hunger, thirst, curiosity, and fatigue.
- **Pause** or press **Space**. **Speed** cycles through 0.5×, 1×, and 2×.
- The camera follows the focused living Norn. Move the observer camera with **WASD** or the arrow keys to suspend follow; selecting a Norn or using guidance resumes it. Use the mouse wheel to zoom.
- Use **Save**, **Load**, or **Reset** in the HUD.
- Pair the focused adult or elder with another eligible living adult or elder in **Family and eggs**, then choose **Start family egg**. The family panel shows each egg's incubation progress and the living-plus-egg population count.
- Use **Record video** to capture the running habitat, including the HUD. Select **Stop recording** to finalize the local AVI; use **Open recordings** to browse saved clips.

The save file is `user://albia_norn_save.json`, inside Godot's per-project user data directory. Version-7 saves include every living or deceased family profile, lineage, life state, partial incubation, cognition, biochemistry, language, feedback, focus, simulation time, and the shared mark surface. Version-1 through version-6 saves migrate with their existing learning and chemistry intact; versions before 6 start with a blank mark surface. Profiles without family metadata receive deterministic founder defaults. No cloud service, remote model, runtime network access, speech input, or speech output is used by the Godot slice. The browser prototype remains the reference for voice features.

## Mark making

The first mark-making slice adds one shared board and a charcoal tool to Sunmeadow. A Norn can consider drawing only while both objects are in its ordinary line-of-sight and 10-unit sensing range and the tool is available. Curiosity supplies a small exploratory drive; the motor is a deterministic seed- and recurrent-state prior, not learned drawing skill. Urgent hunger and fatigue continue to take priority, and making a mark gives no drawing-specific reward.

The board stores a 256×256 raster and at most 256 stroke sequences with 128 points each. Every 0.2 simulated seconds, a nearby Norn can add one short point step with bounded pressure and contact. The 16×16 perception input samples local normalized marks from the same image used by the board and HUD preview. Completed `draw-mark` episodes record the author, board, charcoal tool, timestamp, point count, and endpoints; point geometry stays on the shared surface. No mark is rated for beauty or symmetry, and marks are not interpreted as symbols, mood reports, or social messages.

The cognition input has 282 values: 20 existing named signals, 256 mark values at indices 20–275, two target-relative drawing cues at indices 276–277, and four appended cues at indices 278–281 for thirst, nearby forage, nearby water, and nearby exploration. Existing input positions and action IDs stay stable. Cognition state version 5 preserves the earlier seeded recurrent policy and existing action weights, then independently seeds the appended cue columns and forage, drink, and explore action heads. Older cognition versions migrate to version 5 with bounded zero thirst and neutral associations for the new actions. Outer save version 7 stores surface strokes and the four most recently used quantized cursors. A new author evicts the least recently used cursor while retaining all marks. A full representative save with 256×128 points and four cursors measured 1,104,062 UTF-8 bytes in the earlier version-4 format; this historical size is not a fresh version-7 measurement. World save versions 1–5 load with a blank mark surface; version 6 retains its saved surface while migrating to version 7.

Run the drawing slice assertions with `Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --headless --path .\godot --script res://tools/verify_drawing_system.gd`. The rendered walkthrough is `Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --path .\godot --script res://tools/record_drawing_preview.gd`; it checks approach, incremental contact, texture perception, save/load, reset clearing and reloading, and the normal habitat view. It snapshots and restores the local save, then writes its AVI and extracted sample frames under `user://recordings/expanded-habitat-review/drawing/`; the top-level `user://recordings/` clip count is unchanged.

The seeded mark-making baseline holds chemistry steady, keeps the surface and charcoal eligible, and sets the draw association to 1 so it measures the bounded exploratory motor path rather than action acquisition. It compares one Norn against two for 60 simulated seconds; the first Norn keeps seed 9901 in both runs, and the second uses seed 9902. The one-Norn run produced 39 strokes from 300 decisions, with a 0.974 natural closure rate, 0.001404 mean endpoint quantization error, 0.557 motif recurrence, and 0.359 recurrence after shuffled point-order controls. The two-Norn run produced 77 shared-surface strokes from 600 decisions, with a 0.974 natural closure rate, 0.001299 mean endpoint quantization error, 0.668 mean per-Norn recurrence, and 0.479 mean shuffled-control recurrence. Endpoint quantization error here means the distance between a continuous cursor command and its stored 8-bit endpoint. This exploratory mark-making baseline has no target; the separate reach baseline below measures endpoint accuracy under an explicit target task.

### Single-point reach baseline

The isolated reach evaluator gives a Norn the target-relative cursor delta and asks it to reach one of eight points at radius `0.24` UV from the nominal center. Direction 0 is right/east, then directions 1–7 proceed clockwise in screen coordinates. Each trial begins with a blank 256×256 surface and nominal cursor `(0.5, 0.5)`, which persists on the surface's 255-interval grid as `(128/255, 128/255)`. The delta is recomputed from that quantized cursor at each step, divided by `0.25` UV, and clamped to `[-1, 1]`; the target remains separate from the 16×16 mark input.

Every trial runs six motor decisions at `0.2` simulated seconds each. The evaluator applies only the motor output through `DrawingSurface.append_point()`, including pen-up steps, and scores the final quantized cursor as `distance(final_uv, target_uv) × 255` pixels. A hit is an endpoint within 8 pixels, inclusive. Chemistry and traits stay fixed during the trial; each trial gets fresh hidden state, while the same seed regenerates its prior motor parameters. No reward, action-association, or motor-weight updates occur.

The no-update baseline uses four trials in each direction for each seed. These five policy seeds produced:

| Seed | Trials | Median endpoint error (pixels) | Hit rate |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 9901 | 32 | 61.204832 | 0.00% |
| 9902 | 32 | 64.921352 | 0.00% |
| 9903 | 32 | 60.463091 | 0.00% |
| 9904 | 32 | 64.480978 | 0.00% |
| 9905 | 32 | 74.009924 | 0.00% |
| **Median across seeds** | **32 per seed** | **64.480978** | **0.00%** |

This measures the current seeded motor prior under a fixed target protocol. The baseline includes all eight directions. A later learning comparison may train on cardinal directions 0, 2, 4, and 6, then evaluate on held-out diagonals 1, 3, 5, and 7; that study is outside this implementation. This baseline adds no learning rule and does not establish learned motor skill, biological learning, or intent. Run the checks with `Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --headless --path .\godot --script res://tools/verify_drawing_reach.gd` and print a fresh baseline with `Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --headless --path .\godot --script res://tools/evaluate_drawing_reach.gd`.

The motif descriptor bins each stroke by one of eight endpoint directions, four endpoint displacement ranges, and four point-count ranges. Recurrence is the fraction of same-individual stroke pairs with matching descriptors. The shuffled control permutes each stroke's point order in 64 seeded replicates, then averages the same within-individual score. This coarse descriptive comparison does not establish learned motifs or representation. The measured full-save growth was 10,045 bytes for one active drawer and 20,169 bytes for two. Chemistry was held constant, so state-to-mark correlation was not estimated and no mood claim follows. This baseline does not justify a learning-progress or social-copying experiment: it uses a fixed draw association, no target, and does not align each observer's local sample window with the drawer's window.

## Family and lifespan

Each Norn's life advances in simulation time, so pause and the 0.5×, 1×, and 2× speed controls affect aging and egg incubation along with the rest of the world. The deterministic stages are **Baby** from birth to 60 seconds, **Child** from 60 to 180 seconds, **Adolescent** from 180 to 420 seconds, **Adult** from 420 to 3,000 seconds, and **Elder** from 3,000 seconds until death. Seeded lifespans range from 3,300 to 3,900 simulated seconds, about 55 to 65 minutes at 1× speed. Old age is the only implemented cause of death; reaching the lifespan removes the living actor while preserving the deceased profile and family history.

Life stages also affect behavior. A baby follows its nearest living parent and settles at the usual peer distance when its needs do not choose fruit, the spinner, or rest; player guidance takes priority. Children choose a visible parent first when they decide to observe a peer and learn 1.5 times the adult amount from witnessed food, play, Praise, or Scold. Adolescents score visible peers after normal cognition selects peer observation: proximity contributes `clamp(1 - distance / 10, 0, 1)`, non-parents add `0.25`, and remembered positive or negative `observe` / `observe-feedback` episodes add up to `±0.35`. Social memory uses recorded valence and salience, fades with a 180-simulated-second half-life, and saturates after three recency-weighted memory units. The highest score wins, with distance then actor ID as deterministic tie-breakers. Guidance and fruit, spinner, or rest decisions keep their priority; babies, children, adults, and elders keep their existing peer rules. Observation still requires the normal attention, range, and line-of-sight checks.

Living adults and elders can pair with another living adult or elder. Pairing creates one 45-simulated-second egg, records both parents, and deterministically derives the child's name, seed, traits, and blended body color. Living Norns plus eggs share a limit of four. Eggs continue incubating if a parent dies. When a Norn dies, focus moves to another living family member when one exists; a read-only archive retains deceased relatives and lineage. Eggs can continue when no Norn is alive. When both the living roster and egg list are empty, the extinction panel preserves the archive until the player explicitly starts a new run.

## Biochemistry

Each Norn owns 18 bounded concentrations from 0 to 100: the ten cognition signals plus starch, glycogen, pyruvate, ATP, ADP, oxygen, carbon dioxide, and water. Eating, toy play, Praise, Scold, word teaching, and nearby peers add named inputs. Data-authored reactions digest and store fuel, produce or use energy, recover fatigue, and decay short-lived drives and feedback signals. The biochemical core stays local and deterministic; `AlbiaWorld` advances each profile every 0.1 simulated seconds, synchronizes the original ten signals into that Norn's cognition, and caps catch-up at five chemistry steps per rendered frame.

The **Biochemistry** HUD button opens the 340-by-600 read-only inspector without resizing the existing HUD. Concentrations appear in stable registry order. The lower section lists the six largest non-zero fluxes from the latest step. The inspector is for observing the simulation and does not edit chemical values.

Video recordings are saved under `user://recordings/` as local Motion JPEG AVI files at 1280×720 and 15 FPS. They contain video only. Godot's viewport is sampled while the simulation continues, including while paused; no external encoder or network service is required. The **Open recordings** button opens the local output folder. The game keeps at most 10 completed AVI clips; after a new clip is finalized, it removes the oldest filename timestamps until the folder is back under the limit.
